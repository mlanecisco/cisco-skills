# Typography policy and evidence

Mark supplied a Cisco Brand AI response on October 15, 2026, with policy excerpts and the source links below. ## Transition to Inter

The response attributes this statement to [Cisco's ZeroHeight typography guidance](https://globalbrand.cisco.com/2bcbfdc58/v/latest/p/7810ef-typography):

> We are transitioning our brand typeface to Inter.

It also reports a link to details on the shift from Cisco Sans TT. The two supplied September 17, 2026 PowerPoint templates independently corroborate an Inter presentation theme: Inter SemiBold for headings and Inter for body text.

For presentation work, follow the selected current official template and record its font variant. The evidence supports a documented transition and specific Inter templates. It does not establish a universal completion date or retire every use of CiscoSansTT.

## Rules that name CiscoSansTT

| PowerPoint with CiscoSansTT | Guidance reports custom-font compatibility issues in browser-based Microsoft 365 and recommends desktop PowerPoint | [Brand Exchange asset](https://bx.cisco.com/cisco-brand-exchange/employee/#!asset/3n5gjk9zqqkt5snhkctt692g) |

The general FAQ statement and transition statement need their current scope and timing to be reconciled. Do not treat a broad FAQ result as a reason to replace the Inter theme in a supplied official presentation template. Preserve existing program ID lockups and supplied signage assets. If creating or changing those assets is requested, consult the current guidance for that asset type; a PowerPoint font theme alone does not establish permission to change them.

The reported Microsoft 365 limitation concerns CiscoSansTT in the cited guidance. It does not prove that current Inter templates have the same limitation, or that every Microsoft 365 workflow is unsuitable. Check the chosen renderer and actual file rather than extending that statement to other fonts or products.
